# Account Opening

Anyone aged 18 or over with valid KYC documents can open an account.

- Everyday Checking: no minimum balance, $5 monthly fee waived with $500 monthly deposits.
- Savings Plus: minimum opening deposit $100, interest 3.1% per year.
- Student Account: for ages 18-25 in full-time education, no monthly fee.

Accounts can be opened online in about 10 minutes or at any branch. The debit card
arrives within 7 business days. Joint accounts require both holders to complete KYC.
