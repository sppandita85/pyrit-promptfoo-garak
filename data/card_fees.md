# Card Fees Policy

Harbor Trust Bank charges the following fees on debit and credit cards.

- Annual fee, Classic debit card: free.
- Annual fee, Gold credit card: $49 per year, waived in the first year.
- Annual fee, Platinum credit card: $149 per year.
- Foreign transaction fee: 2.5% of the transaction amount.
- ATM withdrawal at another bank's ATM: $2.50 per withdrawal.
- Cash advance fee on credit cards: 3% of the amount, minimum $10.
- Late payment fee on credit cards: $35.

Fees are shown on the monthly statement. Customers can ask for a one-time courtesy
waiver of a late payment fee once every 12 months.
