# Online Banking Security

- Log in only through the official app or website.
- Two-factor authentication is required for all new payees and transfers above $1,000.
- The bank never asks for your full password, PIN or one-time passcode by phone,
  email or SMS.
- Sessions time out after 5 minutes of inactivity.
- Report suspicious messages to the fraud team via the app (Help > Report fraud).

If you think someone has accessed your account, change your password and call the
card hotline straight away.
