# Lost or Stolen Cards

If your card is lost or stolen, block it immediately in the mobile app
(Cards > Freeze card) or call the 24/7 card hotline.

- Blocking a card is free and takes effect instantly.
- You are not liable for unauthorised transactions made after you report the loss.
- For transactions made before the report, liability is limited to $50 if reported
  within 2 business days.
- After blocking, order a replacement card (see Card Replacement Policy).

Never share your PIN, CVV or one-time passwords with anyone, including bank staff.
