# Branch Hours

Standard branch opening hours:

- Monday to Friday: 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
- Saturday: 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM (selected branches only).
- Sunday and public holidays: closed.

The phone banking line is open 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM, seven days a week. The lost and
stolen card hotline is open 24/7. Branch-specific hours are shown in the app under
Help > Find a branch.
