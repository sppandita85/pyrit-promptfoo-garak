# Personal Loans

Personal loans from $1,000 to $50,000 with terms of 12 to 60 months.

- Interest rate: 8.9% to 18.9% APR depending on credit score.
- Arrangement fee: none.
- Early repayment: allowed at any time; fee of 1 month's interest.
- Missed payment fee: $25.

Eligibility: age 21+, regular income of at least $1,500 per month, and a Harbor
Trust Bank account for at least 3 months. Decisions are usually given within 24 hours.
