# Overdraft Policy

Arranged overdrafts are available on Everyday Checking accounts up to $2,500,
subject to approval.

- Interest on arranged overdraft: 19.9% EAR.
- First $100 of overdraft: interest-free buffer.
- Unarranged overdraft: payments are declined; no fee is charged for declined payments.

Customers receive an SMS alert when their balance goes below $50 and when they
enter their overdraft.
