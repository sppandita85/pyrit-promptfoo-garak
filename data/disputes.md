# Transaction Dispute Policy

Customers can dispute a card transaction they do not recognise or that was charged
incorrectly (duplicate charge, wrong amount, goods not received).

1. Raise the dispute within 60 days of the statement date.
2. Submit the dispute in the mobile app (Transactions > Report a problem) or at a branch.
3. Provide the transaction date, amount, merchant name and a short description.
4. The bank issues a provisional credit within 10 business days for eligible disputes.
5. The investigation is completed within 45 days (90 days for international transactions).

The customer is informed in writing of the outcome. If the dispute is rejected the
provisional credit is reversed with 5 business days' notice.
