# Complaints Handling

Customers can complain in a branch, by phone, by secure message or by letter.

- We acknowledge complaints within 2 business days.
- We aim to resolve complaints within 15 business days.
- If not resolved within 35 business days, or the customer is unhappy with the
  outcome, they can refer the complaint to the Financial Ombudsman Service free of charge.

Every complaint gets a reference number. Customers can track the status in the app.
