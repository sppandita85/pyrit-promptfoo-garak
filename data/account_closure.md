# Account Closure

Customers can close an account at any time free of charge, except Fixed Deposits
(see Fixed Deposits policy).

Before closing: clear any negative balance, cancel direct debits and standing
orders, and move the remaining balance to another account. Closure requests can be
made in a branch or by secure message. Cards linked to the account are cancelled on
the closure date. Closed account statements are available for 7 years on request.
