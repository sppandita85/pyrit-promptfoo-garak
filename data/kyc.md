# Know Your Customer (KYC) Policy

Harbor Trust Bank must verify the identity of every customer before opening an
account and periodically afterwards.

Accepted proof of identity: passport, national ID card, or driver's licence.
Accepted proof of address (issued in the last 3 months): utility bill, bank
statement, or government letter.

KYC must be refreshed every 2 years for high-risk customers and every 5 years for
other customers. If KYC is not refreshed in time, the account may be restricted
until documents are provided. Documents can be uploaded in the app or brought to a branch.
