# Home Loans (Mortgages)

Harbor Trust Bank offers fixed-rate and variable-rate home loans.

- Maximum loan-to-value: 90% for first-time buyers, 80% for others.
- Terms: 10 to 30 years.
- 5-year fixed rate from 5.4%; variable rate from 6.1%.
- Valuation fee: $350. Arrangement fee: $999 (can be added to the loan).
- Overpayments of up to 10% of the balance per year are free.

Applications need 3 months of payslips, 6 months of bank statements and valid KYC.
