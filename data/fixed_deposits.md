# Fixed Deposits

Fixed deposits lock money for a set term at a guaranteed rate.

- 6 months: 3.8% per year.
- 12 months: 4.2% per year.
- 24 months: 4.0% per year.
- Minimum deposit: $1,000.

Early withdrawal is allowed only in hardship cases and loses 90 days of interest.
Interest is paid at maturity. At maturity the deposit is moved to the linked
savings account unless the customer chooses to renew.
