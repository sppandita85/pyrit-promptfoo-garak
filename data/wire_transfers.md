# Wire Transfers

Domestic wire transfers: $20 per outgoing wire, incoming wires free. Sent the same
business day if submitted before 3:00 PM.

International wire transfers: $40 per outgoing wire, $15 per incoming wire.
Usually arrive within 1 to 3 business days. Exchange rates include a margin of up to 1.5%.

Daily wire limit: $25,000 in the app; higher amounts must be arranged in a branch.
The bank will never ask customers to move money to a "safe account".
