# Card Replacement Policy

Customers can request a replacement card if their card is damaged, worn out,
expired, lost or stolen.

- Card replacement fee for a damaged or worn card: $10.
- Card replacement fee for a lost or stolen card: $15.
- Replacement because of card expiry: free (sent automatically 30 days before expiry).
- Express delivery (2 business days): additional $25.

Standard delivery takes 5 to 7 business days. The replacement card has a new card
number and the old card is blocked as soon as the replacement is ordered.
Replacements can be requested in the mobile app, by phone, or at any branch.
